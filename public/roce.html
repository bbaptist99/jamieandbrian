Roce Ceremony

The night before our wedding will be our version of a Roce ceremony. In traditional Mangalorean culture (the area of southern India where Brian’s family is from), a day before the wedding, the bride and groom are blessed by friends & family. This is sometimes done with coconut oil, symbolizing cleanliness and prosperity.

We will be having our own version of this ceremony, with a Roce-style party the night before. We invite you all to join, wear traditional Indian attire, and celebrate with us as we begin our lives as husband and wife.

Wedding Day

The wedding day will feature a Jewish-style ceremony in honor of Jamie’s family. We will stand beneath a chuppah, which represents the physical and spiritual home we will build together. There will also be a blessing of the challah in honor of Jamie’s grandfather.

Together these combine our respective heritage and cultures, while representing our life together in New York City.
